Alert level guide

Normal
Rain <25 mm/hr · Water <1.2m
Monitor only. No action needed.
Warning
Rain 25–60 mm/hr · Water 1.2–2.0m
Prepare evacuation. Alert low-lying residents.
Critical
Rain >60 mm/hr · Water >2.0m
Evacuate immediately. Activate CDRRMO.
